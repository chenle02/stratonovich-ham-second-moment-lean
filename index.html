Stratonovich HAM Second Moment: Lean Companion

Lean 4 checks of finite, algebraic and scalar steps in the paper Le Chen, Stratonovich Waves Driven by Space-Time Gaussian Noise: Second Moment and Exact Asymptotics (arXiv:2611.23014).

The book has its own site: https://chenle02.github.io/stratonovich-ham-second-moment/.

The paper studies the Stratonovich hyperbolic Anderson model with temporal covariance |s-t|^(-α₀) and homogeneous spatial covariance of order α. It shows that log E[u(t,x)²] grows on the scale t^κ, with κ = (4-α-α₀)/(3-α), and identifies the limit by a variational formula.

This is a partial formalization. Lean checks the steps listed on the What is checked page. It does not check the paper end to end. The SPDE, the identification of its mild solution with the chaos series, the Gaussian noise of the source and most analytic estimates are outside Lean. Since wave L19 a centered Gaussian field with the source's positive-width covariance, the fixed-order Stratonovich integrals as L^2 limits and the L^2 series u = sum_n X_n are constructed in Lean (model M9), and since wave L20 this u satisfies the mild equation E:mild-construction (P:mild-construction, for every source field; no uniqueness). Where a checked step needs such a fact, the Lean statement takes it as an explicit hypothesis, and the paper's proof supplies it.

The correspondence between the Lean statements and the paper was checked by AI correspondence review (models from a different family than the ledger's author), not by human verification; see Trust.

This release

v0.11.0 is the wave-L15 to wave-L21 gates (waves L15 to L21 since v0.10.0), the end point of the Lean work: after wave L21 the author planned no further proof waves. Its tree is the tree of development commit 9534f89 of the private development repository: the wave-L21 gate commit 38a3e00 with the receipts, Easley evidence and gate records of waves L15 to L21, the comment-only documentation repairs of the wave-L21 reviews and of the re-anchor reviews, and the re-anchor of every manuscript line citation to manuscript revision edf7085f (book 1.1.3), with the cluster evidence in verification/ redacted; no Lean proof file changed after the gate except in comments. The interface records of waves L15 to L20 (docs/l15/ to docs/l20/), which some module docstrings and the statement ledger name as design records, are working notes of the development repository and are not part of this tree. It is one commit on top of v0.10.0's history, followed by a receipts commit. provenance/DEVELOPMENT.md names the development repository and the commit, and lists every change.

What is new: waves L15 to L21 add 70 modules to StratonovichHAMSecondMoment/, 17 coverage rows (118 in all) and 2130 claims (6632 in all). Wave L15 gives the phase space H^(1/2) x H^(-1/2) and L:uniform-E-lift; wave L16 the Cameron-map chain; wave L17 the pairing-envelope chain; wave L18 the spatial Wick measures and the deterministic half of L:joint-fixed-order; wave L19 the L^2 construction on source fields (model M9: a centered Gaussian field with the source's positive-width covariance, the fixed-order Stratonovich integrals and the series u), with P:mixed, L:vertex-crude and L:joint-fixed-order whole; wave L20 the mild construction P:mild-construction; and wave L21 discharges the named input TubeAttained at the manuscript's tubes. The named inputs still supplied at this end point are SignedMultiplierForm, ThreeTransfer, TubeTail and CutoffCompact. Every compared theorem is credited to a coverage row or declared a helper, as in v0.10.0.

The book's release. The line citations are anchored to manuscript revision edf7085f, the book's release 1.1.3: tag v1.1.3 of the book repository (public commit 18eef2e33331; the tag's commit 4fa7e18a5b6d adds the commit map on top). The public history of that repository is rewritten, and its history/commit-map.tsv translates original-history revisions, including the manuscript revisions named in this README, to public commits. Every TeX file of the manuscript (135 files) is byte-identical in edf7085f and in 18eef2e33331, so the line citations read the same in the public history. Version 1 of the arXiv preprint 2611.23014 is the book's release 1.0.0; coverage.yaml pins the release 1.1.3 that the citations follow (commit and SHA-256 of the book PDF at the tag). provenance/manuscript-anchor.json keeps the file hashes of edf7085f.

Commit hashes in this README, in COMPLETION.md, in the statement ledger and in verification/ are commits of the development repository.

Generated pages. The verification page and the coverage page are generated by lean-pkg from the recorded receipts and coverage.yaml of this release. The comparator checks all 3653 theorem claims in two configurations: the 1553 theorems of comparator.json are the declarations of claims that a row of coverage.yaml names (1134 named by the rows at the wave-L13/L14 gate, 419 credited in this release to the row of a direct manuscript label in their statement-ledger row); the other 2100, in comparator-helpers.json, are helper theorems that lean-pkg-signoff.json declares a smoke check: compared like the others, but credited to no result. The verification page reports a result whose row also names definition claims as "not compared by a second checker" (see the note on that page): the comparator compares theorems, not definitions, and every theorem claim of every row is compared. A coverage row says how a result is formalized; it is not a human verification of the correspondence (see Trust and verification).

The pages of this site are rendered from the repository's own files (README.md, COMPLETION.md, FORMALIZATION.md, docs/statement-ledger.md) by site/build.py; those files are the authority, and this site repeats them.
