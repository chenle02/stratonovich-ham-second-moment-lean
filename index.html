Stratonovich HAM Second Moment: Lean Companion

Lean 4 checks of finite, algebraic and scalar steps in the paper Le Chen, Stratonovich Waves Driven by Space-Time Gaussian Noise: Second Moment and Exact Asymptotics (in preparation).

The paper studies the Stratonovich hyperbolic Anderson model with temporal covariance |s-t|^(-α₀) and homogeneous spatial covariance of order α. It shows that log E[u(t,x)²] grows on the scale t^κ, with κ = (4-α-α₀)/(3-α), and identifies the limit by a variational formula.

This is a partial formalization. Lean checks the steps listed on the What is checked page. It does not check the paper end to end. The SPDE, its solution, the Stratonovich integrals, the Gaussian noise and the analytic estimates are outside Lean. Where a checked step needs such a fact, the Lean statement takes it as an explicit hypothesis, and the paper's proof supplies it.

The correspondence between the Lean statements and the paper was checked by AI correspondence review (models from a different family than the ledger's author), not by human verification; see Trust.

This release

v0.10.0 is the wave-L13 and wave-L14 gates (waves L10 to L14 since v0.9.0). Its tree is the tree of development commit 09cfc11 of the private development repository: the gate commit 8f51004 with its receipts, its Easley evidence and its gate record, two claims that named Lean-generated declarations dropped, the comparator split in two configurations (below), release documentation, and the re-anchor of every manuscript line citation to manuscript revision 39f2597a, with the cluster evidence in verification/ redacted; no Lean proof file changed after the gate except in comments (the re-anchored line citations). It is one commit on top of v0.9.0's history, followed by a receipts commit. provenance/DEVELOPMENT.md names the development repository and the commit, and lists every change.

What is new besides waves L10 to L14: every compared theorem is accounted for. 1553 theorems are credited to rows of coverage.yaml: the 1134 that the rows named at the gate, and 419 whose statement-ledger label cell names a direct manuscript label with a coverage row (credited to that row in this release). The other 2100 helper theorems are compared in a separate configuration that lean-pkg-signoff.json declares a smoke check, and the 491 of them that were marked reusable are now marked internal in claims.yaml. With that, lean-pkg renders the generated verification and coverage pages, which the documentation site now carries.

The book's release. The book is released as tag v1.0.0 of the book repository, original-history revision 86cf977b (public commit 51a02011ba40; the tag's commit 643d1dcc8911 adds history/commit-map.tsv on top). The public history of that repository is rewritten, and its history/commit-map.tsv translates original-history revisions, including the manuscript revisions named in this README, to public commits. One of them, 39f2597a, has no public commit: it is a merge that added only a private record to its first parent 74d2ec31, so the public history keeps that parent (public commit a6fc8b9baaca) and drops the merge. Every TeX file of the manuscript is byte-identical in 39f2597a and in a6fc8b9baaca, so the line citations anchored to 39f2597a read the same in the public history. The book's front matter differs from 39f2597a: a line under the title on the title page, and a new Preface paragraph on responsibility and authorship, with one figure moved. No line of the title page or the Preface is cited here. Every other change between the two revisions to a TeX file that this companion cites replaces lines one for one and touches no cited line. So every manuscript line citation here, anchored to 39f2597a, cites the same line numbers and the same text in 86cf977b. No Lean file, receipt or claim changed; provenance/manuscript-anchor.json keeps the file hashes of 39f2597a.

Commit hashes in this README, in COMPLETION.md, in the statement ledger and in verification/ are commits of the development repository.

Generated pages. The verification page and the coverage page are generated by lean-pkg from the recorded receipts and coverage.yaml of this release. The comparator checks all 3653 theorem claims in two configurations: the 1553 theorems of comparator.json are the declarations of claims that a row of coverage.yaml names (1134 named by the rows at the wave-L13/L14 gate, 419 credited in this release to the row of a direct manuscript label in their statement-ledger row); the other 2100, in comparator-helpers.json, are helper theorems that lean-pkg-signoff.json declares a smoke check: compared like the others, but credited to no result. The verification page reports a result whose row also names definition claims as "not compared by a second checker" (see the note on that page): the comparator compares theorems, not definitions, and every theorem claim of every row is compared. A coverage row says how a result is formalized; it is not a human verification of the correspondence (see Trust and verification).

The pages of this site are rendered from the repository's own files (README.md, COMPLETION.md, FORMALIZATION.md, docs/statement-ledger.md) by site/build.py; those files are the authority, and this site repeats them.
