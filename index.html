Stratonovich HAM Second Moment: Lean Companion

Lean 4 checks of finite, algebraic and scalar steps in the paper Le Chen, The second moment of the hyperbolic Anderson model (in preparation).

The paper studies the Stratonovich hyperbolic Anderson model with temporal covariance |s-t|^(-α₀) and homogeneous spatial covariance of order α. It shows that log E[u(t,x)²] grows on the scale t^κ, with κ = (4-α-α₀)/(3-α), and identifies the limit by a variational formula.

This is a partial formalization. Lean checks the steps listed on the What is checked page. It does not check the paper end to end. The SPDE, its solution, the Stratonovich integrals, the Gaussian noise and the analytic estimates are outside Lean. Where a checked step needs such a fact, the Lean statement takes it as an explicit hypothesis, and the paper's proof supplies it.

The correspondence between the Lean statements and the paper was checked by AI correspondence review (models from a different family than the ledger's author), not by human verification; see Trust.

This release

v0.9.0 is the first public release. Its tree is the tree of the wave-L9 gate of the private development repository (development commit 34b65d7, a docs-only successor of the verified commit 6eb79c7), with release documentation added and the cluster evidence in verification/ redacted; no Lean file and no other build input changed. provenance/DEVELOPMENT.md names the development repository and the commit, and lists every change. Waves L10 to L14 are in development and not part of this release (see COMPLETION.md, "In progress"); v1.0.0 is planned after they are gated.

Commit hashes in this README, in COMPLETION.md, in the statement ledger and in verification/ are commits of the development repository.

The generated coverage and verification pages come with v0.10.0. lean-pkg renders a package's verification and coverage pages only when every theorem judged by the recorded comparator receipt is credited to a row of coverage.yaml. In v0.9.0 the comparator judged all 2050 theorem claims, and 1408 of them are helper theorems that no coverage row names yet, so lean-pkg 0.29.0 refuses to render those pages. The helpers will be mapped in v0.10.0; they are not attributed to results here. Until then the status of each labelled result is in the completion record and the statement ledger, and coverage.yaml is the machine-readable map of the covered results.

The pages of this site are rendered from the repository's own files (README.md, COMPLETION.md, FORMALIZATION.md, docs/statement-ledger.md) by site/build.py; those files are the authority, and this site repeats them.
