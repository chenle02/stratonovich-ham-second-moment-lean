Trust and verification

The generated coverage and verification pages come with v0.10.0. lean-pkg renders a package's verification and coverage pages only when every theorem judged by the recorded comparator receipt is credited to a row of coverage.yaml. In v0.9.0 the comparator judged all 2050 theorem claims, and 1408 of them are helper theorems that no coverage row names yet, so lean-pkg 0.29.0 refuses to render those pages. The helpers will be mapped in v0.10.0; they are not attributed to results here. Until then the status of each labelled result is in the completion record and the statement ledger, and coverage.yaml is the machine-readable map of the covered results.

Trust

A successful build checks that the proofs are correct for the statements as written in Lean. Whether a Lean statement says what the paper needs is a separate question. The statement ledger answers it row by row and is meant to be read next to the paper. Lean does not check that correspondence.

Correspondence review, in short. Each wave's ledger rows were reviewed against the manuscript by AI models from a different family than the ledger's author, and the author accepted every wave, the last at the wave-L9 gate. The full record follows.

Verification record

This repository. A receipt names the commit it binds, so the receipts for this repository's root commit cannot be part of that commit: a lower-tier receipt and a comparator receipt are cut at the root commit and added, with nothing else, by the commit after it, which the release tag v0.9.0 marks. They are the files in .lean-receipts/ whose 12-hex prefix is the root commit (git rev-list --max-parents=0 HEAD). The other receipts in .lean-receipts/ bind commits of the development repository and are kept as development evidence.

Development record.

Commit 6eb79c7 (the wave-L9 gate) has both lean-pkg receipts in .lean-receipts/ (earlier commits have their own): a lower-tier receipt (a fresh build of all modules and the axiom report, 0 sorry, only the standard axioms, and the axioms of all 2468 claims read from the built environment) and a comparator receipt (Palomar's toolchain mechanism): every one of the 2050 theorem claims is judged against its own restatement in Challenge.lean, whose types are checked to be equal to the claimed declarations' by scripts/comparator/CheckFidelity.lean. With both, lean-pkg claims ledger reports no open claim. Later commits that change only documentation stay bound to it. A receipt records the build and the statements' self-consistency; it does not by itself establish that the statements match the paper (see Trust).
