Trust and verification

Generated pages. The verification page and the coverage page are generated by lean-pkg from the recorded receipts and coverage.yaml of this release. The comparator checks all 3653 theorem claims in two configurations: the 1553 theorems of comparator.json are the declarations of claims that a row of coverage.yaml names (1134 named by the rows at the wave-L13/L14 gate, 419 credited in this release to the row of a direct manuscript label in their statement-ledger row); the other 2100, in comparator-helpers.json, are helper theorems that lean-pkg-signoff.json declares a smoke check: compared like the others, but credited to no result. The verification page reports a result whose row also names definition claims as "not compared by a second checker" (see the note on that page): the comparator compares theorems, not definitions, and every theorem claim of every row is compared. A coverage row says how a result is formalized; it is not a human verification of the correspondence (see Trust and verification).

Trust

A successful build checks that the proofs are correct for the statements as written in Lean. Whether a Lean statement says what the paper needs is a separate question. The statement ledger answers it row by row and is meant to be read next to the paper. Lean does not check that correspondence.

Correspondence review, in short. Each wave's ledger rows were reviewed against the manuscript by AI models from a different family than the ledger's author, and the author accepted every wave, the last at the wave-L21 gate. The full record follows.
