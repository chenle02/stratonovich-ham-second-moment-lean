**Generated pages.** The [verification page](verification.html) and the
[coverage page](coverage.html) are generated by lean-pkg from the recorded
receipts and `coverage.yaml` of this release. The comparator checks all 3653
theorem claims in two configurations: the 1553 theorems of `comparator.json`
are the declarations of claims that a row of `coverage.yaml` names (1134
named by the rows at the wave-L13/L14 gate, 419 credited in this release to
the row of a direct manuscript label in their statement-ledger row); the
other 2100, in `comparator-helpers.json`, are helper theorems that
`lean-pkg-signoff.json` declares a smoke check: compared like the others, but
credited to no result. The verification page reports a result whose row also
names definition claims as "not compared by a second checker" (see the note
on that page): the comparator compares theorems, not definitions, and every
theorem claim of every row is compared. A coverage row says how a result is formalized; it is
not a human verification of the correspondence (see [Trust and verification](trust.html)).
