What is checked

What is checked

24 modules in StratonovichHAMAudit/, each one step of the paper's proofs (one of them, HermiteWordFock, applies a step to the Fock model and gives the one-mode special case of a labelled result), and 115 modules in StratonovichHAMSecondMoment/. Counted by labelled result (Theorem, Proposition, Lemma, Corollary), they give 44 whole results, in which every conclusion is a Lean conclusion under the manuscript's hypotheses alone; one whole result with two named inputs supplied (P:tube-rayleigh-transfer); 16 narrower results with the manuscript's hypotheses, each with its missing part stated; and steps, parts or narrower forms of 34 further results with named inputs supplied. Counted by module, the ledger marks 40 modules "whole result", 48 "narrower result" and 27 "foundation" or "proof steps" (the foundations: a wave-kernel and time-fractional covariance slice with spectral homogeneity and the damped-norm finiteness, finite Gaussian integration by parts, a Gaussian Schwartz test, weak gradients and a variational floor, elementary series bounds, the density of test functions in the weak-gradient H^1, the finite Wick (Isserlis) formula, the normalized Hermite polynomials and the finite Gaussian Fock layer, the wave Green measures and their Fourier transforms, the integral-form space-time occupation identities, the spatial white-noise model, and the proof steps of the static interaction, interval coefficient, static phase and fixed split bounds and of the Green regularization of the terminal Wick lemma; from waves L13 and L14 the deterministic two-chain diagram model and its d = 1 positions form, the time integrals of the diagram bounds, the white-noise limit of the d = 1 diagrams, an in-domain control of a phase-contraction step, and the weak-gradient H^1 identified with Mathlib's Sobolev space).

  • 372 refutations in refutations/, which go further: each one is a Lean proof that a specified variant of a declaration is false: a manuscript scalar step, a Lean-route-only construction, or a control of totalized arithmetic or of a broader generalization (the ledger states each one's domain).
  • 137 audit files in Audit/, which print the full types and axioms of the public declarations and run consumer examples.

Typical content: parameter ranges for κ and related exponents, exact rational and square-root inequalities, polynomial positivity certificates, small matrix identities, finite counting of Hermite-word coefficients, and integral comparisons whose integrability inputs are hypotheses.

The statement ledger maps each declaration to the manuscript label it supports, says what it states, and names the inputs that stay outside Lean. FORMALIZATION.md gives the overall scope.

What is not checked

  • the existence, uniqueness and chaos expansion of the solution;
  • Stratonovich integrals, Wick pairings beyond finite families of Gaussian values, and the Gaussian noise measure (only finitely many independent standard Gaussians, the finite-dimensional centered Gaussian laws of GaussianComparison and SourceTube, the finite Wick formula of FiniteWick and the finite Hermite and Fock layer of GaussianHermite and HermiteFock are in Lean);
  • the imported bounds from the earlier literature;
  • the Laplace-transform-to-time growth conversion and the variational asymptotics;
  • three of the 35 development modules: normalized Gaussian coordinates, vector Gaussian projection and colored vector words. They depend on a Gaussian-chaos Lean library that is not yet public. Their 9 facts were checked in the private development record but cannot be reproduced from this repository, and nothing here relies on them. They will be added when that library is released.
