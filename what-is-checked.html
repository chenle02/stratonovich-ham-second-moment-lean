What is checked

What is checked

24 modules in StratonovichHAMAudit/, each one step of the paper's proofs (one of them, HermiteWordFock, applies a step to the Fock model and gives the one-mode special case of a labelled result), and 76 modules in StratonovichHAMSecondMoment/. Counted by labelled result (Theorem, Proposition, Lemma, Corollary), they give 25 whole results, in which every conclusion is a Lean conclusion under the manuscript's hypotheses alone; 18 narrower results with the manuscript's hypotheses, each with its missing part stated; and steps or parts of 30 further results. Counted by module, the ledger marks 27 modules "whole result", 31 "narrower result" and 18 "foundation" or "proof steps" (the foundations: a wave-kernel and time-fractional covariance slice with spectral homogeneity and the damped-norm finiteness, finite Gaussian integration by parts, a Gaussian Schwartz test, weak gradients and a variational floor, elementary series bounds, the density of test functions in the weak-gradient H^1, the finite Wick (Isserlis) formula, the normalized Hermite polynomials and the finite Gaussian Fock layer, the wave Green measures and their Fourier transforms, the integral-form space-time occupation identities, and the proof steps of the static interaction, interval coefficient and static phase bounds).

Counts:

  • 2050 public theorems and 509 public definitions. All 2050 theorems (1203 reusable, 847 helper lemmas) and 418 of the definitions are the checked facts listed in claims.yaml.
  • Every public declaration of the proof library depends only on Lean's standard axioms (propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound). There is no sorry in it; Challenge.lean, the comparator's statement file, restates each compared theorem with sorry by design (see Verification record).
  • 68 negative controls in controls/. Each one states a wrong variant (a wrong constant, sign or range, or a dropped hypothesis), and Lean must reject it for a mathematical reason. They show the checked statements are sharp enough to exclude the plausible mistakes.
  • 235 refutations in refutations/, which go further: each one is a Lean proof that a specified variant of a declaration is false: a manuscript scalar step, a Lean-route-only construction, or a control of totalized arithmetic or of a broader generalization (the ledger states each one's domain).
  • 101 audit files in Audit/, which print the full types and axioms of the public declarations and run consumer examples.

Typical content: parameter ranges for κ and related exponents, exact rational and square-root inequalities, polynomial positivity certificates, small matrix identities, finite counting of Hermite-word coefficients, and integral comparisons whose integrability inputs are hypotheses.

The statement ledger maps each declaration to the manuscript label it supports, says what it states, and names the inputs that stay outside Lean. FORMALIZATION.md gives the overall scope.

What is not checked

  • the existence, uniqueness and chaos expansion of the solution;
  • Stratonovich integrals, Wick pairings beyond finite families of Gaussian values, and the Gaussian noise measure (only finitely many independent standard Gaussians, the finite-dimensional centered Gaussian laws of GaussianComparison, the finite Wick formula of FiniteWick and the finite Hermite and Fock layer of GaussianHermite and HermiteFock are in Lean);
  • the imported bounds from the earlier literature;
  • the Laplace-transform-to-time growth conversion and the variational asymptotics;
  • three of the 35 development modules: normalized Gaussian coordinates, vector Gaussian projection and colored vector words. They depend on a Gaussian-chaos Lean library that is not yet public. Their 9 facts were checked in the private development record but cannot be reproduced from this repository, and nothing here relies on them. They will be added when that library is released.
