What is checked

24 step modules in StratonovichHAMAudit/ (the 25th file there, HermiteWordConsumer, has no public declaration: private definitions and examples that exercise HermiteWord, so it is not counted as a step), each one step of the paper's proofs (one of them, HermiteWordFock, applies a step to the Fock model and gives the one-mode special case of a labelled result), and 185 modules in StratonovichHAMSecondMoment/. Counted by labelled result (Theorem, Proposition, Lemma, Corollary), they give 59 whole results, in which every conclusion is a Lean conclusion under the manuscript's hypotheses alone; one whole result with inputs supplied (P:tube-rayleigh-transfer, one named input since wave L21); 22 narrower results with the manuscript's hypotheses, each with its missing part stated; and steps, parts or narrower forms of 30 further results with inputs supplied. Counted by module, the ledger marks 49 modules "whole result", 69 "narrower result" and 67 "foundation" or "proof steps" (the foundations: a wave-kernel and time-fractional covariance slice with spectral homogeneity and the damped-norm finiteness, finite Gaussian integration by parts, a Gaussian Schwartz test, weak gradients and a variational floor, elementary series bounds, the density of test functions in the weak-gradient H^1, the finite Wick (Isserlis) formula, the normalized Hermite polynomials and the finite Gaussian Fock layer, the wave Green measures and their Fourier transforms, the integral-form space-time occupation identities, the spatial white-noise model, and the proof steps of the static interaction, interval coefficient, static phase and fixed split bounds and of the Green regularization of the terminal Wick lemma; from waves L13 and L14 the deterministic two-chain diagram model and its d = 1 positions form, the time integrals of the diagram bounds, the white-noise limit of the d = 1 diagrams, an in-domain control of a phase-contraction step, and the weak-gradient H^1 identified with Mathlib's Sobolev space; from wave L15 the phase space H^(1/2) x H^(-1/2) on the manuscript's Fourier norm, with the density of real tests, the duality on tests and the multipliers of bounded smooth functions; from wave L16 the Sobolev scale H^s on Fourier coordinates with the statement shapes of the Cameron-map chain, compactness of finite-rank limits, adjoints and L^2-kernel operators, the compact spectral theorem with positive square roots, and the proof steps of the temporal mean; from wave L17 the label-set two-chain model M7 of the pairing envelope with the statement shapes of the pairing-envelope chain, the density of the Green-smoothed d = 3 wave kernel, and the proof steps of the envelope (convolution norms, regional half-bound, cap removal) and of its application (the bridge from the diagram model M4 and the edge norms of damped and smoothed Green kernels); from wave L18 the gap-coordinate Wick-measure model M8 with the statement shapes of the Wick-measure chain, and the proof steps of the spatial Wick measures (the spectral form and positive Laplace formula, the per-edge bridge to M7, the uniform mass bound by the envelope route, the scaling, the finite-measure Laplace argument) and of the joint temporal tail); from wave L19 the source-field model M9 with the statement shapes of the source-field chain, the isonormal Gaussian field on a real Hilbert space and the feature map of the source covariance (the constructed instance), and the proof steps of P:mixed (the positive-width Wick formula for two width families, the chain reversal, the fixed-order mixed moments at common widths) and of L:joint-fixed-order (the L^2 construction and the Green width removed first)).

Typical content: parameter ranges for κ and related exponents, exact rational and square-root inequalities, polynomial positivity certificates, small matrix identities, finite counting of Hermite-word coefficients, and integral comparisons whose integrability inputs are hypotheses.

The statement ledger maps each declaration to the manuscript label it supports, says what it states, and names the inputs that stay outside Lean. FORMALIZATION.md gives the overall scope.
