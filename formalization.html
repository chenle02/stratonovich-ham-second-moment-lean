Formalization scope

Formalization scope

The paper's argument and where Lean enters

The paper bounds and identifies the growth of log E[u(t,x)²] for the Stratonovich hyperbolic Anderson model. The argument has three layers.

  1. Stochastic and analytic layer. The chaos expansion, the two-chain pairing integrals, Wick pairings (beyond finite families of Gaussian values), Gaussian integration (beyond finitely many standard Gaussians), imported bounds, Laplace transforms and the variational asymptotics. Not in Lean, except a few slices: the Laplace transforms of the wave kernel and of the two-time kernel and the subordination formula (ClockCopy and its foundation modules), the Gaussian transform of the spectral measure (MaskedSpectralGrowth), the uniform scalar frequency row (FrequencyRow), the temporal completion H_a (TimeCompletion), the auxiliary mask norm (MaskedAuxiliaryNorm), the Gaussian heat envelope of the covariance (VariationalMean), a finite Hilbert-valued Bessel inequality (PhaseBessel), the wave Green measures G_s for d = 1, 2, 3 (WaveGreen), the spectral identity and positive definiteness of Gamma = delta_0(dx_1) dx_2 (QuotientObstruction), a finite Gaussian comparison under the positive semidefinite order (GaussianComparison), the two wave variance directions in spectral form (WaveTwoVariances), the variational floor and the Rayleigh-form rate (VariationalFloor, VariationalRate), the deterministic Fourier bound L:spatial-fourier-bound for the smooth spatial ratio and the static controls (SpatialFourierBound, SpatialSmoothRatio, SpatialStaticControls, SpatialOccupationCompleted), series types and the continuity of the growth types in the temporal exponent (SeriesType, TypeContinuity), the white-noise bound formulas and clock transforms (WhiteRelativeWidth, WhiteClockLaplace), the coefficient bound of the interval inventory product (InventoryProduct), an abstract L^2 limit of a regularized series under a uniform scaled moment bound (UniformSeriesTail), the density of test functions in the weak-gradient H^1 (H1Density), the physical four-linear extension and the spatial energy (SpatialFourLinear), the full physical occupation class (OccupationPhysicalClass; both completed in SpatialOccupationL7Completed), the scalar and Hilbert-space steps of the static interaction and interval coefficient bounds (IntervalScalar, IntervalScalarCompleted), the spectral-mass identity R_Gamma(1) = R_alpha (SpectralMassIdentity), Gaussian smoothing as a Fourier multiplier (DampedMultiplier), the positivity of the space-time occupation form, the deterministic occupation ceiling and the spatial bracket (SpaceTimePositivity, OccupationCeilingFinite, SpatialBracket, completed in L8Completed), the masked rates (RemoveMask), the finite Wick formula and the terminal-chain Wick formula with the chain measures given by their masses (FiniteWick, TerminalWick), an abstract compact Young baseline (SymplecticFree) and the Cauchy steps of the static phase orders (StaticPhaseCauchy), the Fourier transforms of the wave Green measures in d = 1, 2, 3 and Archimedes' law for the sphere measure (WaveGreenFourier), the low-alpha convolution bound and the static contraction bounds (LowAlphaConvolution, PhaseContractions, completed in L9Completed), the exact occupation constant and ceiling (RawOccupationEnergy, OccupationCeilingExact), the normalized Hermite polynomials and the finite Gaussian Fock layer with values in a Hilbert factor (GaussianHermite, HermiteFock) and their consumers L:fock-form, L:interval-contractions and E:occupied-multiplication (FockForm), and the scalar and numerical-range steps of the complex phase bounds (ComplexPhaseScalar).
  2. Reductions to explicit inequalities. At many points the analytic argument reduces to a finite statement: an exponent range, an exact rational or square-root inequality, a polynomial that must stay positive on a region, a determinant or matrix inverse, a count of coefficients in a finite word, or an integral comparison once integrability is known. These are what Lean checks.
  3. Assembly of the main theorems from the layers above. Not in Lean.

So each module checks one reduction in layer 2. Its hypotheses state the facts that layer 1 supplies (for example positivity of a parameter, integrability of a function, or an inequality proved analytically in the paper). Its conclusion is the fact that the paper then uses.

Heading categories (the same three are used for the statement ledger's section headings; its Index by label points from each label to those sections with their category, and states nothing per declaration). Labelling convention of the ledger's section tables: every backticked manuscript label in a label cell is an assignment and creates an Index pointer; a label mentioned only as context goes in the "What it states" cell as "(Context: ...)"; a "no direct label" cell contains no manuscript label. The ledger Scope keeps the model-choice register. M1, its only entry, is the d = 3 wave Green measure (s/(4 pi)) map (omega -> s omega) volume.toSphere (wave L6, decision 4); its identification with the surface measure of the TeX is not proved. A registered standard choice preserves whole status only when the result's section names the model (as LowAlphaConvolution, PhaseContractions and L9Completed do for M1); a missing construction (the noise W, an abstract ground family, chain measures fixed by their masses, abstract random paths, an abstract moment function) makes the result narrower. (A) "whole result": every conclusion of the labelled result is a Lean conclusion under the manuscript's hypotheses, possibly with named supplied inputs that are disclosed (coverage class same-assumptions or inputs-supplied, conclusion full; at present every whole result has the manuscript's hypotheses alone); no other module carries it. (B) "narrower result: ...": a labelled result some of whose conclusions are not Lean conclusions, or are proved for a modelled object (such as the class O by joint representatives, or the rate in Rayleigh form and the cost in integral form) whose identification with the manuscript's object is not formalized; the heading names what is missing. (C) "foundation: ..." or "proof steps of ...": facts used by labelled results, not a labelled result themselves; the StratonovichHAMAudit step modules are of this kind except HermiteWordFock, a narrower result.

Modules are counted by their ledger heading; a module that also proves pieces of other results is counted once. Completion modules (ClockCopyHomog, MaskedContinuousWaveCompleted, L9Completed, SpatialOccupationCompleted, SpatialOccupationL7Completed, IntervalScalarCompleted, L8Completed) discharge named inputs of other modules; discharging an input does not make a narrower result whole, so L8Completed (narrower results, including L:uniform-series-tail with the chain measures fixed only by their masses) and the spatial and occupation modules remain "narrower result". ClockCopyHomog, MaskedContinuousWaveCompleted and TimeSchur, which have no section of their own, count as whole-result modules; DampedNorms, SpectralHomog, TimeFractional, WaveKernel, GaussianIBP, GaussianTest and WeakSobolev as foundations.

These counts describe the package. They are not a percentage of the paper. The 25 whole results and 18 narrower results with the manuscript's hypotheses are deterministic lemmas, propositions and corollaries used inside the analytic argument, and the occupation ceiling T:intrinsic-occupation-ceiling (a narrower result: both forms with the exact constant, for S_c^pos; C_2 <= U_2 not formalized); the other three main theorems are not formalized.

Reading a statement

For each declaration, check three things against the paper:

  1. the hypotheses match what the paper has available at that point;
  2. the conclusion is what the paper uses next;
  3. parameter ranges, strict versus non-strict inequalities and quantifier order agree.

The statement ledger records this for every row. Audit/<module>/ prints the full elaborated types, so the statements can be read without opening an editor.

Not yet included

Three development modules use a Gaussian-chaos library that is not yet public: normalized Gaussian coordinates, vector Gaussian projection and colored vector words. They are checked in the private development record and will be added here when that library is released.
