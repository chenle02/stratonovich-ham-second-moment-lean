Formalization scope

The paper's argument and where Lean enters

The paper bounds and identifies the growth of log E[u(t,x)²] for the Stratonovich hyperbolic Anderson model. The argument has three layers.

  1. Stochastic and analytic layer. The chaos expansion of the solution (beyond the source-field model M9 of SourceInterface, wave L19: a centered Gaussian field with the source's positive-width covariance, the fixed-order Stratonovich integrals X_n as L^2 limits and the L^2 series u = sum_n X_n, which since wave L20 satisfies the mild equation E:mild-construction (P:mild-construction; no uniqueness, and no identification with an independently specified solution of the SPDE)), the two-chain pairing integrals (beyond the deterministic diagram model DiagramModel, register entry M4, the label-set model M7 of PairingInterface, the gap-coordinate Wick-measure model M8 of WickInterface, the positive-width pairing integrals of M9, and the diagram bounds and deterministic diagram limits built on them), Wick pairings (beyond finite families of Gaussian values), Gaussian integration (beyond finitely many standard Gaussians and the isonormal field of SourceIsonormal), imported bounds, Laplace transforms and the variational asymptotics. Not in Lean, except a few slices: the Laplace transforms of the wave kernel and of the two-time kernel and the subordination formula (ClockCopy and its foundation modules), the Gaussian transform of the spectral measure (MaskedSpectralGrowth), the uniform scalar frequency row (FrequencyRow), the temporal completion H_a (TimeCompletion), the auxiliary mask norm (MaskedAuxiliaryNorm), the Gaussian heat envelope of the covariance (VariationalMean), a finite Hilbert-valued Bessel inequality (PhaseBessel), the wave Green measures G_s for d = 1, 2, 3 (WaveGreen), the spectral identity and positive definiteness of Gamma = delta_0(dx_1) dx_2 (QuotientObstruction), a finite Gaussian comparison under the positive semidefinite order (GaussianComparison), the two wave variance directions in spectral form (WaveTwoVariances), the variational floor and the Rayleigh-form rate (VariationalFloor, VariationalRate), the deterministic Fourier bound L:spatial-fourier-bound for the smooth spatial ratio and the static controls (SpatialFourierBound, SpatialSmoothRatio, SpatialStaticControls, SpatialOccupationCompleted), series types and the continuity of the growth types in the temporal exponent (SeriesType, TypeContinuity), the white-noise bound formulas and clock transforms (WhiteRelativeWidth, WhiteClockLaplace), the coefficient bound of the interval inventory product (InventoryProduct), an abstract L^2 limit of a regularized series under a uniform scaled moment bound (UniformSeriesTail), the density of test functions in the weak-gradient H^1 (H1Density), the physical four-linear extension and the spatial energy (SpatialFourLinear), the full physical occupation class (OccupationPhysicalClass; both completed in SpatialOccupationL7Completed), the scalar and Hilbert-space steps of the static interaction and interval coefficient bounds (IntervalScalar, IntervalScalarCompleted), the spectral-mass identity R_Gamma(1) = R_alpha (SpectralMassIdentity), Gaussian smoothing as a Fourier multiplier (DampedMultiplier), the positivity of the space-time occupation form, the deterministic occupation ceiling and the spatial bracket (SpaceTimePositivity, OccupationCeilingFinite, SpatialBracket, completed in L8Completed), the masked rates (RemoveMask), the finite Wick formula and the terminal-chain Wick formula with the chain measures given by their masses (FiniteWick, and the white moment bounds for S_2^pos (WhiteModel, WhiteMomentBounds), the fixed degree-split estimate on a finite Fock model (FixedSplitCore, FixedSplit), the multi-mode finite word cutoff and the identification of the interval coefficients Q_(r,q) (FiniteWordCutoff), the spatial column bound on a Fourier-side model (FockColumnBound), the static interaction bounds with fewer supplied inputs (StaticInteractionCompleted), and the constructed terminal-chain measures with their Green regularization (TerminalWickInputs, TerminalWickRegularization), the terminal Wick lemma in full for them and the operator form of the Fock sandwich bound on a finite Fock model of the cutoff Hilbert space (FockSandwichModel).
  2. Reductions to explicit inequalities. At many points the analytic argument reduces to a finite statement: an exponent range, an exact rational or square-root inequality, a polynomial that must stay positive on a region, a determinant or matrix inverse, a count of coefficients in a finite word, or an integral comparison once integrability is known. These are what Lean checks.
  3. Assembly of the main theorems from the layers above. Not in Lean.

So each module checks one reduction in layer 2, except that the wave-L19 modules also construct, in Lean, the source-field series of model M9 (a slice of layer 1), and wave L20 proves that this series is a mild solution in the source's sense (P:mild-construction, read as E:mild-construction; no uniqueness is asserted, so its identification with a solution of the SPDE given otherwise stays open). A module's hypotheses state the facts that layer 1 supplies (for example positivity of a parameter, integrability of a function, or an inequality proved analytically in the paper). Its conclusion is the fact that the paper then uses.

(A) "whole result": every conclusion of the labelled result is a Lean conclusion under the manuscript's hypotheses, possibly with named supplied inputs that are disclosed (coverage class same-assumptions or inputs-supplied, conclusion full; at present every whole result has the manuscript's hypotheses alone except P:tube-rayleigh-transfer, which has two named inputs, one (TubeTail) since wave L21); no other module carries it. (B) "narrower result: ...": a labelled result some of whose conclusions are not Lean conclusions, or are proved for a modelled object (such as the class O by joint representatives, or the rate in Rayleigh form and the cost in integral form) whose identification with the manuscript's object is not formalized; the heading names what is missing. (C) "foundation: ..." or "proof steps of ...": facts used by labelled results, not a labelled result themselves; the StratonovichHAMAudit step modules are of this kind except HermiteWordFock, a narrower result.

The 59 whole results and 22 narrower results with the manuscript's hypotheses are lemmas, propositions and corollaries used inside the analytic argument (mostly deterministic; among the exceptions L:terminal-wick and the wave-L19 results stated for every source field of the model M9), and the occupation ceiling T:intrinsic-occupation-ceiling (a narrower result: both forms with the exact constant over the class O itself, for S_c^pos; C_2 <= U_2 not formalized); of the other three main theorems, T:white-moment-bounds is a narrower result (for S_2^pos in place of C_2, with the energy value of P:white-spatial-energy supplied), and T:full-upper and T:intrinsic-second-moment are not formalized.

Reading a statement

For each declaration, check three things against the paper:

  1. the hypotheses match what the paper has available at that point;
  2. the conclusion is what the paper uses next;
  3. parameter ranges, strict versus non-strict inequalities and quantifier order agree.

The statement ledger records this for every row. Audit/<module>/ prints the full elaborated types, so the statements can be read without opening an editor.
