Formalization scope

Formalization scope

The paper's argument and where Lean enters

The paper bounds and identifies the growth of log E[u(t,x)²] for the Stratonovich hyperbolic Anderson model. The argument has three layers.

  1. Stochastic and analytic layer. The chaos expansion, the two-chain pairing integrals (beyond the deterministic diagram model DiagramModel, register entry M4, and the diagram bounds built on it), Wick pairings (beyond finite families of Gaussian values), Gaussian integration (beyond finitely many standard Gaussians), imported bounds, Laplace transforms and the variational asymptotics. Not in Lean, except a few slices: the Laplace transforms of the wave kernel and of the two-time kernel and the subordination formula (ClockCopy and its foundation modules), the Gaussian transform of the spectral measure (MaskedSpectralGrowth), the uniform scalar frequency row (FrequencyRow), the temporal completion H_a (TimeCompletion), the auxiliary mask norm (MaskedAuxiliaryNorm), the Gaussian heat envelope of the covariance (VariationalMean), a finite Hilbert-valued Bessel inequality (PhaseBessel), the wave Green measures G_s for d = 1, 2, 3 (WaveGreen), the spectral identity and positive definiteness of Gamma = delta_0(dx_1) dx_2 (QuotientObstruction), a finite Gaussian comparison under the positive semidefinite order (GaussianComparison), the two wave variance directions in spectral form (WaveTwoVariances), the variational floor and the Rayleigh-form rate (VariationalFloor, VariationalRate), the deterministic Fourier bound L:spatial-fourier-bound for the smooth spatial ratio and the static controls (SpatialFourierBound, SpatialSmoothRatio, SpatialStaticControls, SpatialOccupationCompleted), series types and the continuity of the growth types in the temporal exponent (SeriesType, TypeContinuity), the white-noise bound formulas and clock transforms (WhiteRelativeWidth, WhiteClockLaplace), the coefficient bound of the interval inventory product (InventoryProduct), an abstract L^2 limit of a regularized series under a uniform scaled moment bound (UniformSeriesTail), the density of test functions in the weak-gradient H^1 (H1Density), the physical four-linear extension and the spatial energy (SpatialFourLinear), the full physical occupation class (OccupationPhysicalClass; both completed in SpatialOccupationL7Completed), the scalar and Hilbert-space steps of the static interaction and interval coefficient bounds (IntervalScalar, IntervalScalarCompleted), the spectral-mass identity R_Gamma(1) = R_alpha (SpectralMassIdentity), Gaussian smoothing as a Fourier multiplier (DampedMultiplier), the positivity of the space-time occupation form, the deterministic occupation ceiling and the spatial bracket (SpaceTimePositivity, OccupationCeilingFinite, SpatialBracket, completed in L8Completed), the masked rates (RemoveMask), the finite Wick formula and the terminal-chain Wick formula with the chain measures given by their masses (FiniteWick, TerminalWick), an abstract compact Young baseline (SymplecticFree) and the Cauchy steps of the static phase orders (StaticPhaseCauchy), the Fourier transforms of the wave Green measures in d = 1, 2, 3 and Archimedes' law for the sphere measure (WaveGreenFourier), the low-alpha convolution bound and the static contraction bounds (LowAlphaConvolution, PhaseContractions, completed in L9Completed), the exact occupation constant and ceiling (RawOccupationEnergy, OccupationCeilingExact), the normalized Hermite polynomials and the finite Gaussian Fock layer with values in a Hilbert factor (GaussianHermite, HermiteFock) and their consumers L:fock-form, L:interval-contractions and E:occupied-multiplication (FockForm), the scalar and numerical-range steps of the complex phase bounds (ComplexPhaseScalar), the Riesz spatial maxima in d = 2, 3 (HigherRieszTwo, HigherRieszThree), the degree-one convolution bound (AlphaOneConvolution, completed in L10Completed), the white-noise model and the white moment bounds for S_2^pos (WhiteModel, WhiteMomentBounds), the fixed degree-split estimate on a finite Fock model (FixedSplitCore, FixedSplit), the multi-mode finite word cutoff and the identification of the interval coefficients Q_(r,q) (FiniteWordCutoff), the spatial column bound on a Fourier-side model (FockColumnBound), the static interaction bounds with fewer supplied inputs (StaticInteractionCompleted), and the constructed terminal-chain measures with their Green regularization (TerminalWickInputs, TerminalWickRegularization), the terminal Wick lemma in full for them (TerminalWickComplete), the spectral measure of the smoothed covariance (DampedSmoothing), the finite Gaussian linear-image comparison, the half-action identity and the Rayleigh transfer (SourceTube), and the operator form of the Fock sandwich bound on a finite Fock model of the cutoff Hilbert space (FockSandwichModel).
  2. Reductions to explicit inequalities. At many points the analytic argument reduces to a finite statement: an exponent range, an exact rational or square-root inequality, a polynomial that must stay positive on a region, a determinant or matrix inverse, a count of coefficients in a finite word, or an integral comparison once integrability is known. These are what Lean checks.
  3. Assembly of the main theorems from the layers above. Not in Lean.

So each module checks one reduction in layer 2. Its hypotheses state the facts that layer 1 supplies (for example positivity of a parameter, integrability of a function, or an inequality proved analytically in the paper). Its conclusion is the fact that the paper then uses.

Labelling convention of the ledger's section tables: every backticked manuscript label in a label cell is an assignment and creates an Index pointer; a label mentioned only as context goes in the "What it states" cell as "(Context: ...)"; a "no direct label" cell contains no manuscript label. M1 is the d = 3 wave Green measure (s/(4 pi)) map (omega -> s omega) volume.toSphere (wave L6, decision 4); its identification with the surface measure of the TeX is not proved. M2 (wave L11) is the Gaussian space L^2(Omega; X) over finitely many coordinates as normalized Hermite coefficient vectors, with finite sums and integrals of words taken coordinatewise (used by FiniteWordCutoff, and by FockSandwichModel for its cutoff Hilbert space); the identification of a Bochner integral with the coordinatewise integral is not proved. M3 (wave L12, accepted by the author on 2026-11-13) is the smoothed covariance p_eps * Gamma of L:damped-homogeneous-norms as the measure with Lebesgue density gamma_eps(x) = int p_eps(x - y) Gamma(dy) (used by DampedSmoothing); its identification with Mathlib's Measure.conv of N(0, eps I) and Gamma is not proved. M4 (wave L13; accepted by the author on 2026-11-13) is the deterministic two-chain diagram model DiagramModel (diagram integrals with the Green factors as measures of the increments, and for a covariance measure the limit of common-width smoothings); its identification with the positions form is proved in d = 1 only, and its identification with the TeX's joint limit at arbitrary vertexwise widths is not proved; the common-width convergence of the diagrams is the named input DiagramLimits and, with the finiteness of the limits, a clause of the named input MixedMoment (the statement of P:mixed); the diagram limits are proved for white noise in d = 1 and the order-two Riesz limit in RieszFourDiagrams. M5 (wave L14; accepted by the author on 2026-11-13) realizes H_t (x) G, H_t, H_I and H_Gamma as L^2 completions under the kernel and covariance forms (OccupationCompletion, SourceHeatGram, and the codomain H_sp of Q in ClassOH1); no tensor identification and no isometry between H_sp and H_Gamma is proved. M6 (wave L14; accepted by the author on 2026-11-13) is H^1 as the graph of the weak gradient in L^2 x L^2 (ClassOH1.H1R), with its topology proved equal to that of Mathlib's H^1; not proved: that every real element of Mathlib's H^1 comes from it. (A) "whole result": every conclusion of the labelled result is a Lean conclusion under the manuscript's hypotheses, possibly with named supplied inputs that are disclosed (coverage class same-assumptions or inputs-supplied, conclusion full; at present every whole result has the manuscript's hypotheses alone except P:tube-rayleigh-transfer, which has two named inputs); no other module carries it. (B) "narrower result: ...": a labelled result some of whose conclusions are not Lean conclusions, or are proved for a modelled object (such as the class O by joint representatives, or the rate in Rayleigh form and the cost in integral form) whose identification with the manuscript's object is not formalized; the heading names what is missing. (C) "foundation: ..." or "proof steps of ...": facts used by labelled results, not a labelled result themselves; the StratonovichHAMAudit step modules are of this kind except HermiteWordFock, a narrower result.

Modules are counted by their ledger heading; a module that also proves pieces of other results is counted once. Completion modules (ClockCopyHomog, MaskedContinuousWaveCompleted, L9Completed, SpatialOccupationCompleted, SpatialOccupationL7Completed, IntervalScalarCompleted, L8Completed, L10Completed) discharge named inputs of other modules; discharging an input does not make a narrower result whole, so L8Completed (narrower results, including L:uniform-series-tail with the chain measures fixed only by their masses) and the spatial and occupation modules remain "narrower result" (so do UniformSeriesTail and L8Completed for L:uniform-series-tail, which is whole in TerminalWickInputs, a narrower-result module because of L:terminal-wick, which is whole in TerminalWickComplete); AlphaOneConvolution, whose two named inputs L10Completed discharges, proves every conclusion of L:alpha-one-convolution and is a whole result. HigherRieszTwo (the d = 2 clause and the steps shared with d = 3) is a narrower-result module; HigherRieszThree holds the whole lemma. ClockCopyHomog, MaskedContinuousWaveCompleted and TimeSchur, which have no section of their own, count as whole-result modules; DampedNorms, SpectralHomog, TimeFractional, WaveKernel, GaussianIBP, GaussianTest and WeakSobolev as foundations.

These counts describe the package. They are not a percentage of the paper. The 44 whole results and 16 narrower results with the manuscript's hypotheses are deterministic lemmas, propositions and corollaries used inside the analytic argument, and the occupation ceiling T:intrinsic-occupation-ceiling (a narrower result: both forms with the exact constant over the class O itself, for S_c^pos; C_2 <= U_2 not formalized); of the other three main theorems, T:white-moment-bounds is a narrower result (for S_2^pos in place of C_2, with the energy value of P:white-spatial-energy supplied), and T:full-upper and T:intrinsic-second-moment are not formalized.

Reading a statement

For each declaration, check three things against the paper:

  1. the hypotheses match what the paper has available at that point;
  2. the conclusion is what the paper uses next;
  3. parameter ranges, strict versus non-strict inequalities and quantifier order agree.

The statement ledger records this for every row. Audit/<module>/ prints the full elaborated types, so the statements can be read without opening an editor.

Not yet included

Three development modules use a Gaussian-chaos library that is not yet public: normalized Gaussian coordinates, vector Gaussian projection and colored vector words. They are checked in the private development record and will be added here when that library is released.

Possible next steps

  • the proof of P:mixed (the named input MixedMoment: the Wick formula at positive widths, the joint diagram limits of L:joint-fixed-order and the L^2 convergence of the chaos series), which would turn the diagram-model results of waves L13 and L14 into statements about the solution;
  • a reusable Laplace-to-time growth lemma, with its analytic inputs as hypotheses;
  • the constant and exponent algebra of the main upper and lower bounds.
